# 9. DO NOT CONFUSE THESE FILE TYPES

Keep the distinction clear:

### Android

```text
APK
```

is directly installable on Android.

An:

```text
AAB
```

is primarily for Google Play and is not the same thing as a directly installable APK.

### iOS

An:

```text
IPA
```

is the installable/distributable iOS application artifact, but installation/distribution still depends on Apple signing and the chosen distribution method.

Do not report an AAB as an APK.

Do not report an iOS project/archive as an IPA unless an IPA was actually generated.
