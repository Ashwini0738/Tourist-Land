IMPORTANT:
Do not replace secure authentication with client-side checks.
Do not disable Clerk authentication.

GOAL

The desired authentication flow is:

User enters Email or Mobile Number
        ↓
Clerk authenticates the user
        ↓
Clerk session becomes active
        ↓
Travel & Land backend verifies the authenticated Clerk identity
        ↓
Find or create the Travel & Land user profile
        ↓
Read the role from the server/database
        ↓
Navigate to the appropriate screen

There should be NO organization provisioning step.
